Sec-Agent · Privacy policy

What data the site handles, including Google user data, and how.

ControllerRulesSOCBatch

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Sec-Agent (sec-agent.naurzalinov.me) is a public demonstration of an AI security controller: it checks text and the actions an AI agent proposes for prompt injection and blocks unsafe ones. This policy explains what data the site handles, including data from your Google account if you choose to connect Gmail.

1. What we collect

  • What you enter. The request and document text you type, paste or upload, and the prompts you screen on the Batch page.
  • If you connect Gmail: your Google account email address, and a Google access token that lets the site read your mail. We request only the gmail.readonly scope (plus openid and email) — the site cannot send, delete or change mail.
  • Email content — only the messages you ask for, at the moment you ask (for example "summarize my 3 newest emails", or "Load my Gmail inbox" on the Batch page).
  • Technical logs. The web server records standard request logs (IP address, time, page requested), used only to operate and secure the service.

We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising. Two cookies are set only when you connect Gmail: sa_oauth_state (protects the sign-in, deleted after it) and sa_gmail (a random session id, HttpOnly). The colour-theme choice is kept in your browser's local storage.

2. How we use Google user data

  • Emails are read only when you request it, to show them to you and to check them for prompt injection and unsafe instructions.
  • To perform that check, the text of the emails you ask to check is sent to these processors:
    • Amazon Web Services — Amazon Bedrock (Anthropic Claude models), which runs the AI agent and the AI security reviewer. Under AWS terms, Bedrock does not use your inputs to train models.
    • TypeSafe AI ("Jev"), a prompt-injection risk classifier, which receives the text being scored.
  • We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, use it to build user profiles, or use it to train or improve AI or machine-learning models — ours or anyone else's.
  • No person reads your emails, except where you give explicit permission (for example, when you share a screenshot for support), where needed for security purposes such as investigating abuse, or where required by law.

Sec-Agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Storage and retention

  • Google access token: kept only in the server's memory, never written to disk, and discarded when it expires (about one hour), when you click Disconnect, or when the service restarts. We do not request a refresh token, so the site cannot access your mail after that.
  • Email content: not stored. Emails are held in memory only while your request is processed. Checks you run on an email from Gmail are not written to the security event log, and mail content is never written to the audit trail (it records only "N emails returned").
  • Security event log (the public SOC dashboard): for runs on text you type or paste — not on Gmail content — the site keeps the request, the decision, the checks that fired (which can quote short excerpts of the checked text), and the document's length and a hash, never the full document. Do not paste confidential text into the public demo. The log keeps at most the most recent 20,000 runs.
  • HR runs dashboard (the public HR runs page): Demo HR screening runs on your connected Gmail are recorded there as counts only — how many applications were loaded, flagged, excluded or included, the categories of the security signals, how long review decisions took, and the final decision — and no names, subjects, senders, recipients, reasons or email content. Runs on the synthetic sample inbox are not recorded. The site administrator can delete all recorded runs at any time; the log keeps at most the most recent 5,000 runs.
  • Batch screening: results are returned to your browser and not stored on the server.

4. Your choices

  • Connecting Gmail is optional; everything else works without it.
  • Click Disconnect on the Controller page to end the session and revoke the access at Google. You can also revoke it any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To ask for a security-log entry to be deleted, or for any privacy question, contact us (section 7).

5. Security

The site is served over HTTPS only. Tool credentials and the Gmail access are handled by a separate execution service, and the AI agent never sees your Google token. No security measure is perfect; see the terms for the demo's limits.

6. Children

The service is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
